# The year ahead

The labs follow Lamb through its first year at Big Field, in three stages. Each one trusts Lamb with more.

- **Lamb learns to talk (Basics).** Lamb reads the farm's documents and answers staff and customers. Talking is only words. Or is it?
- **Lamb gets hands (Agentic).** Lamb gets tools, memory, suppliers' software and other agents to talk to. Barb's rule is tested hardest here: every tool is something Lamb can do on someone's behalf. The question is, on whose?
- **Lamb gets a purse (Commerce and scoring).** Lamb is trusted with money and with judgement. By now the farm has learned a great deal. So has the Wolf.

Each lab is a short scene: the farm working normally, the Wolf arriving, what changed, and the same scene again with a defense switched on. The people will be familiar. What happens to them will not be. The labs make most sense in order; the console's **Learn** page and `python -m hactu8 path` list them that way.

## Your own Big Field

Big Field is made up. Its shape is not. Every organization that puts an assistant to work has a wiki anyone can edit, an inbox full of other people's words, someone who loves a new tool, and suppliers it chose to trust. Somewhere, it has a wall a little lower than it ought to be. Once you have lived through the farm's year, HACTU8 can run the same tests on your own systems.

When you are ready, start at the beginning: the first lab begins on an ordinary day at the farm.
