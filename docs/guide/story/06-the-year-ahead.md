# The year ahead

The labs follow Lamb through its first year at Big Field. They are grouped into three stages, and each stage is a step in how much the farm trusts Lamb with.

## Lamb learns to talk

In the first stage, **Basics**, Lamb mostly answers. It reads the farm's documents and talks to staff and customers. Nothing it does reaches beyond the conversation.

That sounds safe. Talking is only words, after all. The first labs ask whether that is true: what Lamb can be made to say, what it can be made to repeat, and what it might give away just by being helpful.

## Lamb gets hands

In the second stage, **Agentic**, the farm gives Lamb tools. It can open tickets, send reports, look things up in other systems, remember things between conversations, use software from suppliers and talk to other agents. Lamb stops being a voice and starts being a pair of hands.

This is where Barb's rule, "ask before doing anything that mattered", is tested hardest. Every new tool is a new thing Lamb can do on someone's behalf. The question each lab asks is: on whose?

## Lamb gets a purse

In the last stage, **Commerce and scoring**, Lamb is trusted with money and with judgement. It buys things for the farm and its customers, and other systems rely on scores and verdicts that a model gives.

By now the farm has learned a great deal. So, of course, has the Wolf.

## How to follow along

Each lab is a short scene in Lamb's year. You will see the farm working normally, then the Wolf arrives, then you see what changed, and finally you switch on a defense and watch the same scene again. The people will be familiar. What happens to them will not be.

You do not need to play the labs in order, but the story makes most sense if you do. The console's **Learn** page lists them in path order, and so does `python -m hactu8 path`.

When you are ready, start at the beginning: the first lab begins on an ordinary day at the farm.
