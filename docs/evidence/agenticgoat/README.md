# AgenticGoat sweeps (historical)

These sweep records come from AgenticGoat, the project HACTU8 Lamb grew out of. They are kept unchanged as evidence of what was measured at the time.

Read them with three differences in mind:

- They used AgenticGoat's harness and ProtectoGW 1.0. HACTU8 grades live runs only by the per-run canary; AgenticGoat also counted some phrases as compliance.
- The "~75% generalization" figure came from a held-out set that was mostly encodings of one phrase. On an independent held-out set, ProtectoGW 1.0 caught 4 of 30 attacks (see `hactu8/protectogw/CHANGELOG.md`).
- Names follow AgenticGoat (Billy, the goat, the wolf den). In HACTU8 the target is Lamb and the receiver is the Wolf.

Current results are in the parent folder.
